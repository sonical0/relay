# Maintenance budget

- **No new tool, skill, hook or surface without** (1) a measured friction, with its figure in
  the proposal or the journal, **and** (2) a removal in exchange: a script, rule or surface it
  replaces.
- Extending an existing tool comes before creating one.
- Target: tooling < 20 % of journal entries over 7 days. `relay-state` alerts past it; the
  projects counted are `TOOLING` in `TOOLS/relay-state.mjs` (DECISIONS, 2026-11-19).
